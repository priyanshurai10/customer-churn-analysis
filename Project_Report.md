# Project Report: Customer Churn Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
For this project, I focused on "Customer Churn", which basically means when a customer stops doing business with a company. It is much cheaper to keep an old customer than to find a new one, so finding out why people leave is a very important business problem.

I built a dashboard that shows the current customer data and also has a prediction model to guess if a new customer will leave or not.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
I chose three main KPIs for the dashboard to show the business health:
- **Total Customers:** To know how big our user base is.
- **Overall Churn Rate:** The percentage of people leaving. This is the most important metric we need to lower.
- **Average Monthly Charges:** To see how much revenue an average customer brings in.

## 3. Data Analysis & Trends
While exploring the data, I found some interesting trends:
- **Support Tickets are a big warning sign:** Customers who opened 3 or more support tickets had a very high chance of leaving. This means they were facing issues and probably didn't get good help.
- **New customers leave more:** People who have been with the company for less than 6 months are more likely to churn compared to long-term loyal customers.

## 4. Risks and Opportunities
Based on the analysis, here is what the business should focus on:

**Risk:** 
We are losing customers because of bad support experiences. If a customer has to complain multiple times, they get frustrated and cancel their subscription.

**Opportunity:** 
If we can identify the unhappy customers early, we can offer them a free month or a discount before they decide to leave. 

**Action Plan:**
1. Use the prediction model (in the app) to scan all current customers.
2. For anyone who is flagged as "High risk of churn", the customer retention team should call them and ask how they can help.
3. Improve the customer service team's response time so people don't have to open multiple tickets.
